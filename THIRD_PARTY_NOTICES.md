# Third-party notices

LanternLM integrates with third-party software and models that are not covered
by the LanternLM trademark notice.

- **llama.cpp** provides the local inference server. Distribution of its
  binaries must retain the notices required by the upstream license.
- **GGUF models** are supplied separately. Each model remains subject to its
  own license and acceptable-use terms.
- Runtime JavaScript packages retain their respective licenses as recorded in
  `package-lock.json`.

Before publishing an installer, include the exact license files for the
llama.cpp build and every bundled model.
